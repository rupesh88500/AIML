Aim: Design a prototype of an expert system
Theory:
REACTOR - Nuclear expert system
REACTOR is a pioneering artificial intelligence program developed by EG&G Idaho, Inc. to help nuclear 
power plant operators diagnose and respond to reactor accidents.

Overview

• Purpose: Designed to assist control room operators during high-stress emergency situations by analyzing plant 
           conditions and recommending treatment.
• Strategies: Combines event-oriented strategies (identifying specific accident sequences) 
             with function-oriented strategies (monitoring critical safety functions like cooling).
• Knowledge Representation: Utilizes response trees to link specific accident scenarios directly 
                             to correct emergency procedures.

Core Components

• Knowledge Base: Stores expert rules, operational safety criteria and physical relationships of nuclear system.
• Inference Engine: Evaluates incoming sensor data against the stored rules to deduce the state of the reactor.
• User Interface: Displays actionable advice and system status to human operators in real time and very clearly.

Advantages & Disadvantages

Advantages:
1. Rapid Diagnosis: Processes data instantly to
2. Objective choice: Remains calm and entirely logical under intense stress.
3. Knowledge Retention: Safeguards expert human wisdom for permanent availability.
4. Dual-strategy Care: Monitors both specific faults and overall core health.
Disadvantages:
1. No Common sense: Fails completely when facing unprogrammed, novel situations.
2. Data Dependency: Faulty sensor readings will trigger dangerous system mistake.
3. High Maintenance: Demands continuous, expensive updating by nuclear experts.
4. No Accountability: Humans must always authorize final critical safety actions.

Conclusion

REACTOR pioneered objective, dual-strategy nuclear accident diagnosis to prevent operator panic. 
While limited by rigid rules and sensor reliance, it proved AI works best as an active safety partner rather 
than a human replacement.
